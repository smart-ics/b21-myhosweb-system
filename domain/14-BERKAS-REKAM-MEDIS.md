# BERKAS REKAM MEDIS DOMAIN

Domain Code: **BRM**

## 1. Purpose

The **Berkas Rekam Medis** Domain manages diagnosis coding, patient morbidity and casemix classification, hospital indicators, regulatory reporting, census and index information, and the movement of physical medical-record files.

It supports the hospital's responsibility to maintain standardized coded information, produce required reports, and make physical medical-record files available to the units responsible for patient care.

## 2. Definition

The **Berkas Rekam Medis** Domain is responsible for classifying documented clinical information for reporting and analysis, producing hospital statistical and regulatory reports, and coordinating the custody and movement of physical medical-record files.

This Domain does not own the original clinical documentation or the operational facts generated by clinical service units. It uses those authoritative records as inputs for coding, classification, statistics, and reporting.

## 3. Responsibilities

The Berkas Rekam Medis Domain is responsible for:

- Completing and maintaining diagnosis coding according to applicable classification standards.
- Classifying patient cases for morbidity analysis and casemix purposes.
- Producing required hospital reports, including RL reports submitted to the government.
- Providing census, index, and hospital indicator information derived from operational records.
- Coordinating the movement and custody of physical medical-record files between the Medical Records unit and care units.

## 4. Capabilities

### 4.1 BRM-CODING — Diagnosis Coding

**Definition**

The capability to assign and maintain standardized codes for diagnoses documented in the patient's clinical record.

**Responsibility**

- Identify diagnoses requiring coding.
- Assign codes according to applicable classification standards, such as ICD-10 where required.
- Maintain the relationship between coded diagnoses and their source clinical documentation.
- Support coding review and correction when the documentation or assigned codes are incomplete or incorrect.

The clinical diagnosis remains the responsibility of the authorized healthcare provider. This capability classifies the documented diagnosis; it does not independently establish or change the clinical diagnosis.

### 4.2 BRM-MORBID — Patient Morbidity and Casemix

**Definition**

The capability to classify patient cases using coded diagnoses and other required case information for morbidity analysis and casemix grouping.

**Responsibility**

- Organize coded diagnoses for morbidity analysis.
- Classify eligible cases using applicable casemix grouping rules.
- Provide classification information required for case-based reporting and applicable health-insurance claim processes.
- Maintain traceability between the classified case, its source clinical information, and its resulting classification.

**Casemix**

Casemix groups patients or episodes of care into categories based on defined clinical and other case characteristics.

Diagnosis coding and casemix are related but distinct:

- **Diagnosis coding** standardizes documented diagnoses using a classification system.
- **Casemix classification** uses coded diagnoses and other required case information to group a patient case according to an applicable classification system.

In Indonesia's national health-insurance context, this includes applicable INA-CBG and, where required, iDRG grouping processes.

This capability covers medical-record coding and classification responsibilities. BPJS-specific claim submission and external e-Klaim integration remain within the relevant BPJS capability.

### 4.3 BRM-INDIKATOR — Hospital Indicators

**Definition**

The capability to provide hospital performance and service indicators derived from authoritative operational and clinical data.

**Responsibility**

- Calculate defined hospital indicators from their underlying source data.
- Provide indicator values for hospital monitoring and reporting.
- Maintain traceability between each indicator and the data or calculation rules used to produce it.

Indicator definitions, calculation rules, and reporting periods must be explicit. Where authoritative operational data exists, manually entered figures should not replace it as the source of truth.

### 4.4 BRM-RL — RL Reporting

**Definition**

The capability to prepare and produce required hospital RL reports for submission to the government, according to applicable reporting requirements.

**Responsibility**

- Produce supported RL report sections for the required reporting periods.
- Derive report values from authoritative operational data and relevant coding, morbidity, census, index, and indicator information.
- Validate report completeness and consistency before submission.
- Present report results in the required format.

RL reports are reporting outputs, not the primary source of hospital operational facts. Census, index, and indicator information may support RL reporting, but RL reporting should not depend on manually maintained copies when authoritative source data exists.

The exact report sections and formats supported must follow applicable government requirements and the system's implementation scope.

### 4.5 BRM-RPT — Census and Index

**Definition**

The capability to provide census and index information for medical-record administration, hospital statistics, and reporting.

**Responsibility**

- Provide census information showing patient or service activity for a defined period and scope.
- Provide patient and service indexes that support retrieval and analysis of recorded hospital activity.
- Support drill-down from summarized figures to underlying operational records where the source data allows it.
- Provide reusable information for RL reports and hospital indicators.

**Census** summarizes patient or service activity for a defined period. Examples include outpatient visits, inpatient census, admissions, discharges, and other supported service counts.

**Index** organizes recorded information by selected reference dimensions, such as patient, diagnosis, or service, so records and patterns can be retrieved and analyzed.

These outputs should be derived from authoritative operational records. This capability should not become a separate data-entry system that duplicates visits, diagnoses, admissions, or discharges.

### 4.6 BRM-MUTASI — Physical Medical-Record File Movement

**Definition**

The capability to coordinate and track the movement and custody of physical medical-record files between the Medical Records unit and hospital units that need them for patient care.

**Responsibility**

- Record requests for physical medical-record files when required.
- Coordinate the dispatch of files to the relevant care unit.
- Track the current custodian or location of each file.
- Record file transfers, returns, and movement history.
- Identify outstanding, overdue, or unreturned files.

This capability concerns the physical medical-record file and its custody. It does not own patient identity, the clinical contents of the medical record, or the operational transfer of patients between care units.

## 5. Domain Boundary

### Owns

- Diagnosis coding and its association with source documentation.
- Morbidity classification and casemix information within the Medical Records responsibility.
- Hospital indicators and derived statistical information.
- RL report preparation and output.
- Census and index information derived from operational records.
- Custody and movement of physical medical-record files.

### Does Not Own

- Patient identity and demographic master data — **Pasien Domain**.
- Original clinical documentation and clinical decisions — the relevant clinical service and documentation responsibilities.
- Patient registration, admission, discharge, and movement between care units — the relevant operational service Domains.
- Original laboratory, radiology, pharmacy, and other service records — their respective Domains.
- BPJS-specific eligibility, claim submission, and external BPJS integration — **BPJS Domain**.
- Hospital financial billing and payment — **Tata Rekening Domain**.

## 6. Relationships

- **Pasien** provides authoritative patient identity.
- **Admission and clinical service Domains** provide visit, admission, discharge, and service activity records.
- **Clinical service Domains** provide the clinical documentation from which diagnoses are coded.
- **BPJS** handles relevant BPJS-specific claim and integration processes; this Domain provides coding and casemix information within its responsibility.
- **Berkas Rekam Medis** derives census, index, indicators, and RL reports from authoritative source records.
- **Clinical service units** receive and return physical medical-record files through the BRM-MUTASI capability.
